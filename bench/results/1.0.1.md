## Memory: the heap of a world

A new world takes 54,856 bytes instead of 54,538 (+318 bytes, averaged over 200 worlds kept alive):
the two counters that the deletes of 1.0.1 read and the closures that capture them. It is a
constant per world, not a cost per entity, pair or query: a pair record, a plain record and an
entity take the bytes they took in 1.0.0, and the bytes per distinct pair and per holder of P8
are unchanged. The heap rows of P8 take the heap before and after the world in KiB, each
rounded, so the constant moves them by 0 to 2 KiB; a row whose noise is one step and that
moved by two is flagged: native target, the heap of the world after it is built (+2 KiB).
The heap after the lifecycle rounds and after the long sessions grows by the same constant as
the built world: nothing accumulates.
