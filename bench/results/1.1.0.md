## Memory: the heap of a world

A new world takes 55,992 bytes instead of 54,861 (+1,131 bytes, averaged over 200 worlds kept alive):
the closure of `world:expect` (about 100 bytes) and its field: the table of the world had 32
fields, and the 33rd doubles its hash part (1 024 bytes). It is a constant per world, not a
cost per entity, pair or query: a pair record, a plain record and an entity take
the bytes they took in 1.0.1, and the bytes per distinct pair and per holder of P8 are
unchanged. The heap rows of P8 take the heap before and after the world in KiB, each rounded,
so a constant of about 1 KiB moves them by 1 or 2 KiB, and a row whose noise is one step and
that moved by two is flagged: native small-empty-retained, the heap after 6000 more frames (+2 KiB).
