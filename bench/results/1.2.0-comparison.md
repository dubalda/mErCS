Every library runs in a process of its own, 5 times in each mode, the order of the

## Method

- Matrix: `bench/scenarios.luau`, n = 131072, `bench/harness.measure` with three repetitions:
  each process reports the minimum time per operation and the median retained memory after
  a full collection. Scenarios that a library does not support are skipped.
- Synthetic frame: `bench/frame_scene.luau`, three batches per query style; each returns the
  median of 300 frames after 60 warm-up frames, from the batch with the lowest minimum frame.
- Long session: the scenario of `bench/leak.luau`, seed 777, 5000 frames, a full collection at
  every 1000-frame checkpoint (the time of a window includes it).
- Probes: the net heap growth of 1000 warmed loops over 200 entities (a weak witness rejects a
  collection inside the interval), the cost of one `OnRemove` hook on a delete, a child moved to
  another parent; the retained memory of the wildcard worlds.
- Basic: `bench/basic.luau`, the four basic benchmarks of jecs (the work of the Benchmarker
  files spawn, despawn, insertion and query), 300 calls of each, median and minimum per call.

Versions: `jecs` = jecs 0.11.0, `jecs_cleanup` = jecs 0.11.0 + cleanup / 600, `previous` = mErCS 1.1.0, `lib` = mErCS 1.2.0, `ecr` = ecr 0.9.0.
