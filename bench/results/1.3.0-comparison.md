# mErCS 1.3.0, mErCS 1.2.0, jecs 0.11.0 and ecr 0.9.0: complete CLI measurements

Measured: 2026-11-07; Windows-11-10.0.26100-SP0 / AMD64 Family 25 Model 117 Stepping 2, AuthenticAMD; Roblox Studio and the Roblox player were open on the
machine during the measurements (their load widens the ranges).
Luau: `luau-lang/luau@0.740.0`, executable SHA-256 `7ea248f7d78405b251e097772463a88fa2da109cf322db7c9c1f27eb0754df71`.
Previous release: `v1.2.0`, commit `708aa8c1d9294c21ac65f5f7e26f8f4b8427e60e`, source SHA-256 `2af91022c642859e8a274429a5cd3d5dcc312b3cd218d4a873eb146c84bde82d`.
Candidate source SHA-256: `7535b7c9eaec18754f0583d10073519df5661948ad4ee065d6b9394d48f68821`.

Every library runs in a process of its own, 5 times in each mode, the order of the
libraries rotated between the runs. Every cell is median [min–max] of those processes. All raw
values, process output, source hashes, commands and the isolated entry points are in
[1.3.0-comparison.json](1.3.0-comparison.json).

The synthetic frame has 10 more repeats of every library in each mode
(15 in all): in the first 5 the median frame of a library varied
from process to process by 2–9 % (the slowest against the fastest), more than
mErCS 1.2.0 and 1.3.0 differed. The samples keep both batches (the supplemental repeats are
numbered from 101).

The matrix has 10 more repeats of every library in each mode
(15 in all): of the 214 rows that compare mErCS 1.2.0 and 1.3.0,
6 moved by more than 5 % from the medians of the first 5 to those of all of them.
The samples keep both batches (the supplemental repeats are numbered from 201).

The same code took 0.97–1.07 of its time in the campaign of 1.2.0
(`bench/results/1.2.0-comparison.json`, the same machine): the median, per profile, of the time
of a row here divided by its time there, "interpreter / native" (mErCS 1.2.0 is the build
`lib` there and `previous` here). Compare the libraries within one campaign.

## Method

- Matrix: `bench/scenarios.luau`, n = 131072, `bench/harness.measure` with three repetitions:
  each process reports the minimum time per operation and the median retained memory after
  a full collection. Scenarios that a library does not support are skipped.
- Synthetic frame: `bench/frame_scene.luau`, three batches per query style; each returns the
  median of 300 frames after 60 warm-up frames, from the batch with the lowest minimum frame.
- Long session: the scenario of `bench/leak.luau`, seed 777, 5000 frames, a full collection at
  every 1000-frame checkpoint (the time of a window includes it).
- Probes: the net heap growth of 1000 warmed loops over 200 entities (a weak witness rejects a
  collection inside the interval), the cost of one `OnRemove` hook on a delete, a child moved to
  another parent; the retained memory of the wildcard worlds.
- Basic: `bench/basic.luau`, the four basic benchmarks of jecs (the work of the Benchmarker
  files spawn, despawn, insertion and query), 300 calls of each, median and minimum per call.

Versions: `jecs` = jecs 0.11.0, `jecs_cleanup` = jecs 0.11.0 + cleanup / 600, `previous` = mErCS 1.2.0, `lib` = mErCS 1.3.0, `ecr` = ecr 0.9.0.
